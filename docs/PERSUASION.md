# Persuasion map (Cialdini), and the rule we keep
Every lever below is true to what Scape AI actually offers. Do not add fake scarcity, fake counters or invented testimonials: they work for a week and cost trust for years.

| Principle | Where it lives | How |
|---|---|---|
| Reciprocity | Every CTA, exit modal, strip | Lead with a free, concrete gift: a written plan (what to build, how long, what it costs) the visitor keeps either way. |
| Commitment and consistency | Exit modal, dock, flow | Ask a tiny question first ("which problem?"), then 4 short steps with a progress bar. A visitor who answers one question keeps going. |
| Social proof | (needs your real data) | Add real client logos, quotes and numbers to the hero and strip once available. Slot: under the hero CTAs. |
| Authority | Process, eval and framework sections | Specifics over adjectives: benchmarks, red-teaming, written scope. Add real credentials and team bios when ready. |
| Liking | Tone, outcomes in the visitor's words | Options phrased as the visitor's own problem ("I cannot tell if my AI is reliable"), not our service names. |
| Scarcity | (only if true) | If you genuinely cap new engagements per month, say so in the CTA. Otherwise leave it out. |
| Loss aversion | Exit modal copy | "You keep the plan either way" removes risk; the cost of doing nothing is implied by the pain-framed options. |
Rules: one interruption at a time, nothing before ~9 s on the page, neutral dismiss text ("Not now"), never shame the visitor for leaving.
